Upstream source and attribution

The model, sampling, intervention, and Omniglot preparation implementation is an external dependency from https://github.com/aadityasingh/icl-dynamics (Strategy Coopetition Explains the Emergence and Transience of In-Context Learning, ICML 2025). The seven original source files and their frozen duplicates are not redistributed in this public release. See UPSTREAM_SETUP.md for the pinned original source and local restoration procedure. Frozen source identities remain in the scientific input specifications. No upstream LICENSE was present in the inspected source; this release does not grant a license over upstream material. Dataset and pretrained-encoder terms continue to apply.
